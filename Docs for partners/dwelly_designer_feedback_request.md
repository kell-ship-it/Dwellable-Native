# dwelly — Quick Design Feedback (No pressure, no formal audit)

Thanks again for being willing to take a look — really appreciate it. This is a much lighter ask than a full audit, just want your honest reactions.

## What dwelly is
A voice-and-text journaling app for Christians. You talk or type about a moment, dwelly has a short back-and-forth with you about it, then turns that conversation into a written journal entry — helping people notice where God's showing up in everyday life instead of it just fading away.

## What I'm looking for
Not a structured write-up — just your gut reactions as you click through. A few sentences per thing that stands out is plenty. Two flavors of feedback are both useful:
- **Something confusing** — a screen or flow where you weren't sure what was happening or what to do next
- **Something that felt off** — spacing, style, tone, anything that didn't feel cohesive with the rest

If you have a thought on how you'd fix something, great, but don't feel obligated to solve it — flagging it is enough.

Figma file: [link]
