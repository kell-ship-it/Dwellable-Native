1. What Pillar 5 Is (in the Formation Intelligence System)

Proposed Formation Intelligence Role:

P5 is the discovery layer.

In the cascade:

  • P0-P4: Foundation, Capture, Encryption, Prayer, Synthesis & Ownership (user's spiritual story emerges)
  • P5: Enables re-discovery of moments through multiple pathways (date, tags, search, filters)
  • P6: Pattern Detection — finds themes across discovered moments
  • P7: Celebration — validates the entire system works

Key Insight: Users can't notice patterns if they can't find moments. P5 enables "dwelling on themes" by letting users re-access and group moments.

2. What P5 Learns About the User

Proposed Inference Layer:

  1. Discovery Patterns: Do they browse by date? Use tags? Search keywords? Full-text search adoption signals power-user engagement.
  2. Theme Emergence: Which tags do they use? Which filters do they select? Tags reveal what categories matter to them.
  3. Temporal Behavior: When do they review? Do they browse recent moments or dwell on older ones? (Signals reflection depth vs. forward momentum)
  4. Search Intent: What keywords do they search? Query patterns reveal what they're trying to understand or remember.
  5. Re-Engagement Trigger: Does search/discovery bring them back? (Key metric for Phase 2 success)

3. What P5 Communicates to the User

Proposed Communications:

  1. "Your moments are findable" — Not lost; searchable by date, tag, keyword, any way you remember them
  2. "Patterns are emerging" — Calendar view + filters reveal clustering (lots of "Anxiety" moments in one week? tags show that)
  3. "You can revisit and reflect again" — Re-discovery enables re-dwelling. Old moments can be prayed over again.
  4. "Your story is growing" — The ability to browse + filter reinforces that you have a history, a library of moments

4. How P5 Prepares P6 (Formation Intelligence / Pattern Detection)

Current Understanding:

P6 cannot exist without P5's discovery layer.

P5 gives users the ability to browse and organize moments by tags/dates. P6 then detects patterns: "I notice you have 7 'Anxiety' moments in May and 3 'Peace' moments. What patterns do you see?"

Without P5's filtering, pattern detection is invisible. With P5's tags + search, patterns become obvious to the user.
