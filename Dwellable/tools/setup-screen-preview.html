Option A — Upfront download (current implementation):
User sees this once immediately after logging in, before they ever record anything. The delay is expected and framed as "setup." They hit the moments list fresh and ready.

Tradeoff: User can't use voice until the download completes. If they're on slow cell data it could take 2–3 minutes. But they only experience this once, ever.
Option B — Deferred to first capture:
User gets into the app instantly after login. On their very first voice recording, after they tap "Save," the download starts mid-flow. The "Capturing your beautiful moment..." screen plays while the model downloads + transcribes in one go.

Tradeoff: The first capture experience feels slow (could be 3–5 min on slow Wi-Fi). User doesn't know why. Risk of them thinking the app is broken.
