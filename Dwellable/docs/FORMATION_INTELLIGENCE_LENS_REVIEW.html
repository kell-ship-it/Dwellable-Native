🧠Formation Intelligence Lens — MVP vs Post-MVP

A cross-cutting framework: Formation happens in every pillar. What do we capture? What do we act on?

Key Principle: MVP collects signals passively across all pillars. Post-MVP uses those signals to personalize prompts, synthesis, and nudges. User experience in MVP is deliberately uniform; Post-MVP is when learning becomes visible through personalization.
