**Date:** April 21, 2026 | **Status:** Phase 2 Strategy Week

## The Phase 1 Gap

Phase 1 proved one thing definitively: **Capture works.** 100% adoption. Users will use a faith-specific tool over generic alternatives when it's frictionless and intentional.

But Phase 1 also revealed the critical gap: **Return and reflection fail.** Zero re-reads. Moments feel "complete" once captured. Users move on. The insight fades.

**This is the gap Phase 2 must close.**

The primary failure is not capture. It is *retention*—keeping moments active and influential long after they occur.

---

## Phase 2 Strategic Theme

### **From Capture to Dwelling: Building Sustained Encounter**

Phase 2 transforms Dwellable from a capture tool into a *dwelling place*—a space where returned moments accumulate weight, spark reflection, and anchor users in patterns of God's presence over time.

Three core pillars guide Phase 2:

### 1. **Visual Return (Gallery)**
Make returning irresistible. A visually inviting gallery surfaces moments by theme, date, or recency—low-friction re-entry that feels rewarding, not obligatory. First reflection nudge: visual patterns themselves.

### 2. **AI-Generated Moment Imagery**
Each moment gets a unique, branded illustration. Humans return to visual cues. Imagery becomes the door back in—a small gift that says "this moment mattered."

### 3. **Reflection Nudges**
Optional, gentle prompts to revisit moments ("Remember this?"). Timed to interrupt anxiety, uncertainty, or doubt with the very moment that was meant to anchor them. Nudges ask questions, never interpret.

---

## Success Definition

**Qualitative:** "I find myself going back to my moments. They help me see patterns in how God shows up."

**Quantitative:**
- Moment return rate: >10% of users revisit moments weekly
- Average session length: +30% (gallery browsing time)
- Reflection engagement: >20% of users interact with nudges
- Retention: >60% of Phase 1 users active in Phase 2

## Out of Scope (Phase 3+)

- Pattern surfacing (themes, semantic search)
- Biblical anchoring or spiritual interpretation
- Social sharing or community features
- Advanced personalization (adaptive nudge timing)

---

## Key Metrics to Track

1. **Return behavior:** Do users re-open the app to view past moments?
2. **Gallery engagement:** Which moments get viewed most? By when?
3. **Nudge effectiveness:** Do reflection nudges increase session length?
4. **Retention cohort:** Phase 1 users still active in Phase 2?

---

## North Star Question

*Are moments becoming anchors that users return to, or artifacts they leave behind?*

Phase 2 succeeds when the answer shifts from "leave behind" to "can't stop returning to."
